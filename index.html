Our mission

We expand access to learning—and the confidence to pursue it.

Fengzheng Cultural Foundation works to make educational opportunity more reachable for students and families facing barriers.

We bring together practical resources, mentorship, academic enrichment, and culturally informed learning. Our goal is not only to meet an immediate need, but to help create durable pathways to education, belonging, and long-term growth.

A legacy of teaching

The Feng Wantao Scholarship

Named in memory of an educator who spent more than 30 years teaching and continued supporting underprivileged children after retirement, the scholarship carries forward a simple conviction: financial circumstances should not close the door on a hardworking student.

Programs

Practical support. Lasting possibility.

We pair direct educational resources with guidance, cultural connection, and financial assistance—because opportunity is strongest when students have more than one kind of support.

01

Mentorship Network

Students connect with volunteer tutors and experienced professionals who can help make academic and career decisions feel more understandable and achievable.

Areas of guidance
  • Academic pathways
  • Career planning
  • Personal development
02

Cultural Education Workshops

Our workshops integrate cultural awareness with academic skill-building. Students are encouraged to think critically, engage fully, and see their lived experience as a source of strength.

What we reinforce
  • Identity and belonging
  • Critical thinking
  • Educational engagement
03

Feng Wantao Scholarship

Merit-based financial assistance supports students with demonstrated financial need, encouraging academic achievement and continued pursuit of knowledge.

At a glance
  • Up to five awards annually
  • $100–$1,000 per award
  • Merit and financial need considered

Hardworking students from low-income families should have meaningful access to higher education.

The legacy behind the scholarship

A teacher’s commitment, carried forward.

Mr. Feng Wantao (1936–2015) devoted more than 30 years to teaching middle and high school students. After retirement, he continued serving others by supporting underprivileged children in Beijing.

Established in his memory, the Feng Wantao Scholarship honors that lifelong dedication to learning by supporting deserving students on their educational journeys.

Application deadlineSeptember 15
Recipient noticeBy December 15
Potential next stepPhone interview
Award range$100–$1,000

Applicants should submit a completed application and required supporting materials by email. Selected finalists may be invited to a phone interview.
